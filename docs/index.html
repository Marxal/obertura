Why I made this

I'm a passionate chess player — not a very good one. I built Obertura to improve my openings after trying a lot of opening tools and never being satisfied with any of them. So I made my own. Now I hope it helps other players too, and that we can improve it together as we get better.

What you can do

Your openings, learned your way.

Build your repertoire

Enter lines your way — by hand on the board, picked from a built-in opening library, pulled from your own games, suggested by the engine, or lifted straight from scouting an opponent.

Train it

Play a line once to confirm it, then drill it. Spaced repetition brings your mistakes back until they stick — never random.

See the whole picture

A visual tree of your openings and every branch.

Know your openings by name

Opening names recognised automatically as you build.

Get a second opinion

Engine evaluation on any position, whenever you want it.

Bring in your games

Import straight from Chess.com and Lichess.

Scout opponents

Import someone's games and see what they actually play, so you can prepare a surprise.

See what really gets played

Real win, draw and loss numbers from millions of online games on any position you reach.

Learn the traps

A curated library of famous opening traps — tap one to drop the trapping line straight onto your board.

Annotate as you go

Mark moves brilliant, dubious or a blunder, and write your own notes on any position.

Beat the clock

Timed 1, 3 and 5-minute drills with your own personal bests.

Track progress

Stats and streaks that follow your learning.

Never lose your work

Export a backup of your whole repertoire and restore it anytime.

Make it yours

Light and dark themes, and multiple piece sets.

Yours, on your phone

Installable like an app, with no app store — and your data stays on your device.
