Settings → Data → Delete your account removes the account and the copy attached to it straight away; you can email me instead if you’d rather.

The full unlock removes that limit for €9 / 99 SEK, paid once. Not a subscription, no renewal, nothing to cancel. It’s tied to your account, so it follows you to any device you sign in on.

I may change the price for new buyers. If you’ve already paid, you’ve already paid — your unlock doesn’t expire and won’t turn into a subscription later.
