Build your repertoire

Create your opening repertoire however you prefer:

  • Add ideas discovered while preparing for an opponent

Why I made Obertura
🤓

I'm a passionate chess player, not a particularly strong one 🤓

After trying countless opening trainers, I always felt something was missing. Most were either too rigid, too complicated, or didn't match the way I wanted to study.

So I built Obertura for myself.

A place to build my own repertoire, train it efficiently, and prepare for real opponents. If it helps other players improve too, even better. 🙌
